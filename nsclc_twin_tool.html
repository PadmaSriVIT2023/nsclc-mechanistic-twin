Research use only. Not a medical device and not for individual treatment decisions. Absolute risks were overestimated in external cohorts until the baseline hazard was re-estimated locally.

Model card

Intended use. Research and education on mechanistic–ML survival modelling in resected NSCLC. Not validated prospectively.

Training data. TCGA LUAD + LUSC, complete cases, overall survival censored at 60 months. Twin parameters from a CT volume-doubling-time meta-analysis (adenocarcinoma 223 d, squamous 140 d) and TRACERx measured volumes; seeding parameters calibrated on TRACERx recurrence only.

External performance (Harrell's C). OncoSG 0.707 (95% CI 0.649–0.763); NCI Sherlock-Lung 0.663 (95% CI 0.588–0.749). Twin features did not add discrimination over clinical variables.

Known limitations. Squamous histology not externally validated; stage II and III have nearly identical twin inputs; treatment not modelled; OS includes non-cancer deaths.
